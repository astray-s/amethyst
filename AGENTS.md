# Amethyst visual source of truth

Before making a visual or brand change, read [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) and
[`docs/brand/AMETHYST_HOMEPAGE_BRAND_SYSTEM.md`](docs/brand/AMETHYST_HOMEPAGE_BRAND_SYSTEM.md).

The approved homepage is the only source of truth for Amethyst brand decisions:

- Use `docs/brand/reference/homepage-approved-2026-09-15.png` as the visual reference.
- Reuse the locked homepage tokens and the named cave and gem assets.
- Do not infer Amethyst's brand from other screens, older screenshots, obsolete stylesheets,
  or unused assets.
- Keep the black, charcoal, white, lilac, and pink-lavender palette. Do not introduce
  cyan/blue gradient endpoints, orange lighting, glass-heavy surfaces, or pedestal imagery
  unless the user explicitly revises the brand system.
