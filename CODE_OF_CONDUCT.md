# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Amethyst a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps our community:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting different opinions, experiences, and skill levels;
- giving and accepting useful feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone else's private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors to reveal health, usage, identity, or device data;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile or in the repository's private reporting options. Do not open a public issue that exposes the people involved.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter as far as practical.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior when choosing a response. Possible responses include a private correction, a warning, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
